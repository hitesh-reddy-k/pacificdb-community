# PacificDB Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

PacificDB Community is an open-source database software project.

## Information We Collect

PacificDB does not directly collect personal information from users who download the PacificDB Community software.

The PacificDB Community software does not intentionally transmit personal information to PacificDB unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

Our website and software downloads may be hosted or distributed using third-party services such as GitHub. Those services may collect technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and usage data according to their own privacy policies.

## How We Use Information

PacificDB does not sell personal information.

Any information voluntarily provided to the PacificDB project, such as through support requests, issue reports, or contact emails, may be used to respond to requests, investigate problems, improve the software, and maintain project security.

## Third-Party Services

PacificDB may use third-party platforms such as GitHub for source-code hosting and software distribution. Use of those services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as PacificDB develops and introduces new services or features.
