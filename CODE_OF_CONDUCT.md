# PacificDB Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and technical work, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project
events, and the [PacificDB Discord](https://discord.gg/67w8ET9Sf2), as well as
when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report community-conduct concerns privately to a PacificDB moderator through
Discord direct message or to a repository maintainer through a private contact
method on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary. Reports will
be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical, and retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
